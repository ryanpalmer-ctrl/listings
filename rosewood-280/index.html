The Penthouse

Eye level with a church steeple, floating over the treetops, with the sunset all to yourself.

This building opened in 2008. The developer went under before the top-floor corner was finished, and it sat as a concrete shell for years. When it finally came together in 2014, it got the Wolf + Sub-Zero kitchen, site-finished hardwoods and 12-foot ceilings — so the newest-feeling home in the building is the one at the very top.

Nearly 2,600 square feet on a single level, tall French windows pulling light from two directions, a gas fireplace for the cold nights, and two private balconies — one for sunrise coffee, one for sunset wine. The primary suite sits at the far end of its own wing, with a walk-in closet and a spa-style bath.

Downstairs is 8.5 acres of gardens under old oaks, a saltwater pool, tennis and pickleball, and a concierge who knows your name. Two deeded garage spaces and a private storage unit come with the unit.
