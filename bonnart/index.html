Watch service

Most of what comes across the counter is watches. A full service means the movement comes out, gets taken apart, cleaned, oiled, and timed, and the case gets new gaskets so it seals again.

Customers mention the turnaround more than anything else.

Custom design

Bring a sketch, a photo, or a stone you already own. Several reviews say custom work here came in close to what the same piece would cost off the shelf.

It usually starts with a conversation at the counter about what you want, what you want to spend, and any stones you'd like to reuse. You see and approve the design before any metal is cut. The piece is made and set in-house, then sized when you pick it up.

Call ahead for a design appointment so there's time to sit down.

Engagement rings and wedding bands

Start with a diamond we source or a stone from a family piece. The setting is built around it, and the band is made to sit flush against it. Heirloom re-sets and anniversary bands too.
