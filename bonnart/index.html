The House

A family name
over the door.

A. Bonnart is family-owned and family-run, with three generations working in the shop. You'll deal with the people doing the work. The person who takes in your watch is often the one who opens the case back.

We've been in Strawberry Hill on Providence Road for years. A lot of what comes across the counter is a parent's watch, a grandmother's stone, or a ring that needs to fit again. We treat those as if they were ours.

The Bench

Fine watches, serviced
and restored.

Watch service is the heart of the shop. We do full mechanical overhauls, regulation, and the quick fixes that keep a daily watch running, and we're known for fast turnaround.

Watch Service

Complete movement service on luxury Swiss and mechanical watches. We clean, lubricate, regulate, and check the timing against spec.

  • Crystals, gaskets & crowns
  • Pressure testing
  • Batteries

Bespoke

Designed with you.
Made here.

Custom work costs less than most people expect, often about the same as something off the shelf. The difference is that it was made for one person. Bring a sketch, a photo, or a stone you already own.

i.

Conversation

Sit down at the counter and tell us what you have in mind. Bring inspiration, your budget, and any stones you'd like to reuse.

ii.

Design

We work out the design together. You'll see it and approve it before any metal is cut.

iii.

The Bench

The piece is made, set, and finished by hand, and checked at every stage.

iv.

Delivery

You pick it up at the shop, sized and polished. We're here to look after it from then on.
