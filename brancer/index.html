The Process

From first conversation to contract

Three clear steps, with no surprises in between.

  1. 1

    Initial Consultation

    We sit down together to walk through how a Brancer home is built, then review your plan (or one of ours), your site, the finishes you're drawn to and an early budget. No plan yet? Our design team will draw one around the way you live.

  2. 2

    Planning & Design

    A room-by-room study of the home: flooring, cabinetry, stone, appliances, fixtures, siding and outdoor spaces. Each detail is reviewed for both structure and beauty, and refined with you before construction drawings begin.

  3. 3

    Final Review

    With your plans approved, we price the home line by line in a complete Bid Proposal: materials, labor, permits and every associated cost. Once you've reviewed and approved it, we sign the Contract to Build.

    Bid proposal: typically 3–4 weeks

Our Story

A family business, rooted in Denver

Bobby started building young, working alongside his father, and founded United Contracting Group at 18. UCG grew into commercial framing and interiors for brands like Chick-fil-A, Bojangles and Darden Restaurants, but Bobby never lost his love for residential work.

In 2017, Bobby and Jessica launched Brancer Homes, a division of UCG, to focus on what they love most: thoughtfully designed homes for families to grow in for generations. A trusted network of subcontractors and vendors stands behind every project.

Away from the job site, you'll find Bobby with Jessica and their two boys, out on the lake or at a campsite.
