Brand Guide · Concept v1

A. Bonnart
Fine Jewelers

Old-world bench, Providence Road address. The identity is built around the watch dial and the monogram stamp. It should feel quiet, exact, and handed down, never flashy.

The AB seal works as a ring-box stamp, bag sticker, and watch-service tag. The gold diamond at 12 o'clock is the only accent. Don't add any others.

Display · Newsreader
Kept, made, by hand.

Regular 400 for headlines. Use italic gold for one key word per headline, and only one. A quiet book serif, close to what you would see engraved on a ring box.

Text · Barlow

Watch service, bespoke design, engagement & bridal, repair.

Labels · Barlow, tracked caps

Voice

Sounds like

  • "Bring it in. We'll tell you what it needs."
  • "Made here, by the family."
  • Short, plain, specific

Never

  • "Luxury redefined," "timeless elegance"
  • Sales or "sale" language
  • Discount badges, stock photos of models

Imagery

Use dark, low-key light with warm metal against deep fabric. Show one object per frame and leave plenty of negative space. No models, no logos, no stock smiles. Replace these with the shop's own bench and case photography once it's shot.
