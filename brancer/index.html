Questions

Before you begin

Where do you build?

Throughout the Lake Norman region, generally within about thirty miles of the lake: Denver, Sherrills Ford, Catawba, Mooresville, Lincolnton and Maiden among them. We are now building in Charlotte as well.

Do I need to own land first?

No. Some clients come to us with a lot in hand, some are under contract on one, and some are still looking. You are welcome to start the conversation at any point.

Do I need a house plan?

How long does pricing take?

Once your plans are finalized and approved, a complete line-by-line Bid Proposal typically takes three to four weeks.

Who helps with finishes and selections?

Our in-house design team. They take the time to learn your style and guide every choice, from cabinetry, countertops and flooring to the cabinet hardware.

The Process

From first conversation to contract

Three clear steps, and no surprises in between.

  1. 1

    Initial Consultation

    The first meeting is where your vision starts to take shape.

    We walk you through how a Brancer home is built and what to expect when you choose us as your builder. Together we review preliminary house plans, whether yours or one from our collection, along with your site or land plat, the interior and exterior finishes you are drawn to, and early budget considerations.

    If you do not have a plan yet, or have not found one you love, our in-house design team will turn your ideas, needs and wishes into a personalized set of plans in the next stage.

    Our goal: you leave this meeting confident, informed and certain you have found the right builder.

  2. 2

    Planning & Design

    A room-by-room study of the home.

    We build on the plan or concept developed from your consultation and take a deep look at every area: flooring in each room, kitchen cabinetry, countertops, appliances, fixtures, exterior siding and patios. Each detail is reviewed both structurally and aesthetically so the design captures what you have in mind.

    This is also your opportunity to give feedback, request changes and refine the design before we create a full set of construction plans.

  3. 3

    Final Review

    A complete number, then a contract.

    Once your plans reflect every adjustment from Planning & Design and you have approved them, we price the home. The result is a comprehensive Bid Proposal with accurate estimates for materials, labor, permitting fees and every associated cost.

    The full proposal is presented to you for review and approval. After your sign-off, we execute the Contract to Build.

    Because of the detail involved, the Bid Proposal typically takes three to four weeks.

Come Prepared

What to bring to your consultation

  • House plans or sketches, if you have them
  • Your site plan or land plat
  • Photos of homes and rooms you love
  • A sense of the finishes you prefer, inside and out
  • The budget range you would like to stay within

None of it is required. Many clients arrive with only an idea, and that is enough to begin.

Design Selections

Designing your home with Brancer

Designing a custom home means making a great many selections. Our in-house design team takes the time to get to know your personal style, so the process feels both successful and enjoyable.

Because selections are one of the most personal parts of building, we encourage you to prepare ahead. Pre-shopping, gathering inspiration and building mood boards will help you decide quickly and confidently.

Our Story

A family business, rooted in Denver

Founded by Bobby and Jessica Tucker, and built on referrals ever since.

Bobby & Jessica Tucker

Homes for families to grow in for generations

At Brancer Homes, we build high-end custom homes and luxury renovations in the Lake Norman area and the communities around it, and now in Charlotte.

Bobby’s passion for building started early, working alongside his father. He founded United Contracting Group at just eighteen. UCG found its success in commercial construction, completing projects for brands like Chick-fil-A, Bojangles and Darden Restaurants, but Bobby never lost his love for residential work.

In 2017, after building their first home together, Bobby and Jessica launched Brancer Homes to focus on what they love most: beautiful, thoughtfully designed homes. Today Brancer offers full customization, top-tier finishes and a highly collaborative experience.

We believe building a home is a deeply personal journey. Many of our projects come through referrals and repeat clients, and we are proud of that.

Bobby Tucker
